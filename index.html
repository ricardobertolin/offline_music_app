About

Loudness follows ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128: K-weighted gated integrated loudness (LUFS), loudness range (LRA) and 4× oversampled true peak. Album gain is recomputed from the gated block histogram of every track in the album, not averaged. Quality detection measures effective bandwidth (spectral cutoff), clipping, crest factor and bitrate. Everything runs locally; the service worker makes the app itself work offline.
